Delete your Critterdex account

Critterdex: Echoes of Bramblewick, made by niabocmil

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open the game and tap the cog (Settings) at the top right of town.
  2. Scroll down to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap it again to confirm.

Without the game

Email critterdexsupport@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and your player code (the # number under your Keeper name, for example #2423421). If you signed in with email, send it from that address. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

Your Keeper, critters, coins, gems, items, mail, friends, garden, Arena and raid results, and auction bids and listings. Critters you discovered stay in the world's Critterdex as "Found by a former Keeper". Gift letters you sent to friends disappear from their mailboxes within 30 days, like all letters. Nothing else is kept.
