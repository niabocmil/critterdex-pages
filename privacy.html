Privacy Policy: Critterdex: Echoes of Bramblewick

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Critterdex: Echoes of Bramblewick ("the game") is made by niabocmil. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to critterdexsupport@gmail.com.

What the game collects

Your account

  • If you sign in with email: your email address.
  • If you sign in with Google: your email address, which Google shares with us when you sign in.
  • If you play as a guest: a random account number. We don't know who you are.

Your game

  • The Keeper name you choose, your player code, your profile picture choice, level and title.
  • Your critters, coins, gems, items, mail, story progress, quests and Critterdex.
  • If a friend invited you: whose invite code you used, so you both get the invite gift.
  • Your daily gift calendar, your friends list, gifts, garden visits, Arena results, Titan Raid results, and Auction House bids, listings and sales.

On your device only (never sent to us)

  • Your settings (sound, graphics, language, reminders) and how far you got in the first-time guide.
  • Phone reminders ("Your egg is ready!") are set up on your phone only. Android asks your permission first.
  • When you share a picture of a critter, the picture is saved in your phone's photos and goes only where you send it.

What other players can see

Your Keeper name, level, title, profile picture, frame and hat, the three critters in your showcase, your garden (if a friend visits), your Arena rating and league, your raid damage rank, and your bids on the Auction House's top-10 lists. Critters you discover show "Found by" with your Keeper name.

Your player code is shown only to you; you share it yourself to add friends or invite someone.

How we use it

Only to run the game: to save your progress, let you play with friends, run the Arena, raids and auctions, and keep the game fair (for example, to stop cheating). The game has no adverts, no analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your information.

Services that help run the game

  • Supabase stores the game's data and handles sign-in. Information travels to it over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • OpenAI draws the picture of a brand-new critter when one is discovered. The game sends only a description of the critter (its species and traits). Nothing about you is sent.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" sign-in, and Google Play delivers the game.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your game while your account exists. Letters in your mailbox disappear after 30 days.

You can delete everything at any time: in the game, open Settings → Delete account and tap it twice. Your Keeper, critters, coins, gems and progress are removed for good. You can also ask us to delete your account by email at critterdexsupport@gmail.com, including if you no longer have the game installed (see the account deletion page: https://niabocmil.github.io/critterdex-pages/delete-account.html). Critters you discovered stay in the world's Critterdex as "Found by a former Keeper".

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and tell players in the game.
